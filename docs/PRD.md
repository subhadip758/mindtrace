# MindTrace Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Product Vision & Tagline

> **“Don’t let AI tell you who you are. Let it help you discover your patterns.”**

MindTrace empowers individuals to understand relationships between daily habits (sleep, screen time, exercise, study) and subjective wellbeing (mood, energy, focus, productivity) using real longitudinal data, statistics, and N-of-1 experimentation.

---

## 2. Core Principles & Non-Goals

### Core Principles
- Real data only (no synthetic production data or synthetic AI scores).
- Scientific honesty: Always distinguish between correlation and causation.
- Privacy & User Ownership: Complete data export and account erasure support.

### Non-Goals
- MindTrace is NOT a diagnostic tool, therapist, or medical device.
- It will NEVER diagnose depression, anxiety, ADHD, or psychiatric conditions.

---

## 3. User Personas & Target Audience
- **Self-Reflectors & Habit Builders**: Individuals seeking data-driven insights into how lifestyle habits affect performance.
- **Quantified Self Researchers**: Users who want to conduct N-of-1 controlled experiments on their own habits.
